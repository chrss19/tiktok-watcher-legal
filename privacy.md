# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

This tool is a private, personal application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

## What data it accesses
Through the TikTok Display API, the tool reads data from the owner's own TikTok accounts only: the list of the owner's videos and, for each video, its view, like, comment and share counts and its publication time. It also reads basic profile information needed to sign in (user.info.basic).

## How the data is used
The data is used only to track the performance of the owner's own videos and to notify the owner. The tool does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok.

## Storage and sharing
Data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

## Access and deletion
The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
